    A marker's point sits on the spot, so the map right there stays visible. In the PDF every pin keeps a large tap area, even at size S.

    Photos on the place's page

    Side by side puts the close-up and the first photo next to each other. Large gives the photo the full width. Small row shows up to four thumbnails and leaves the most room for text. Every photo also gets its own full-screen page with its caption.

    Photo size

    Applies to photos you add. Small (1,200 px) makes photos about half as heavy and still looks good on a phone. Standard (1,600 px) is a good balance. Large (2,400 px) is for photos people will zoom into, and roughly doubles their weight.

    The saved PDF also keeps everything you set up here. Open it with Open map or PDF to change it later.

    Tapping a pin or a name in the saved PDF opens that place's page. Links work in any PDF viewer, including Files and Books on iPhone. The map is saved at full resolution, so people can pinch to zoom into it.
